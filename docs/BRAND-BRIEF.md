# Radiant 34 Brand Brief

## Brand Name

Radiant 34

## Scripture Foundation

Psalm 34:5:

> Those who look to Him are radiant; their faces are never covered with shame.

## Brand Meaning

Radiant 34 is about looking to God and carrying light, confidence, identity, and freedom from shame into everyday life.

The brand should not feel like basic church merch. It should feel like a premium faith-based clothing label that can sit beside serious streetwear and lifestyle brands.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Christians who want wearable faith-based design
- Young adults who like streetwear, art, and meaningful clothing
- People who want subtle but powerful Bible-inspired pieces
- Churches, ministries, and missions looking for modern Christian products

Secondary audience:

- Gift buyers
- Christian event attendees
- Faith-based creators and influencers

## Visual Identity

Core visual words:

- Radiant
- Warm
- Clean
- Premium
- Hopeful
- Bold
- Everyday
- Light-filled

Avoid:

- gloomy visuals
- cheap religious clipart
- overusing crosses or crowns
- placing the logo on every product
- dark designs with no contrast
- cluttered scripture layouts

## Colour Direction

Primary palette:

- Deep black
- Warm cream
- Soft white
- Radiant gold
- Sunrise amber

Secondary palette:

- Sand
- Charcoal
- Muted bronze
- Light beige

## Logo Direction

Preferred logo concept:

- RADIANT wordmark
- Sun rays integrated cleanly
- Number 34 worked into the identity
- Premium typography
- No crown of thorns
- Strong enough for labels, tags, packaging, water bottles, and social media

## Product Design Rule

Every product does not need the logo. Product designs can use:

- Psalm 34:5 text
- radiant faces concept
- sun-ray graphics
- abstract light marks
- minimal 34 symbol
- devotional artwork
- small chest mark plus larger back design

## Brand Promise

Faith made visible through premium everyday design.

## Tagline Options

- Look to Him. Be radiant.
- Never covered with shame.
- Faith made visible.
- Everyday light. Eternal message.
- Wear the light.

## Launch Positioning

Radiant 34 should launch as a focused capsule, not a random product shop.

Recommended launch capsule:

1. Signature Radiant 34 T-shirt
2. Psalm 34:5 hoodie
3. Radiant tank top
4. Bracelet
5. Water bottle
6. Backpack
7. Key chain

## Investor Angle

Radiant 34 is a faith-based lifestyle brand with product, story, community, and mission potential. It can expand into apparel, accessories, devotionals, events, church partnerships, and creator-led campaigns.
