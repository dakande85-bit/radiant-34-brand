# Radiant 34 Website Design Direction

## Standard

This site must feel like a premium fashion label, not a generic religious merchandise page.

## Mood

- Slick
- Warm
- Editorial
- Minimal
- Confident
- Light-filled
- Investor-ready

## Visual System

Use:

- deep black background
- warm cream text
- controlled gold highlights
- radial light glows
- subtle grain
- clean oversized typography
- large negative space
- premium product cards
- scripture used with restraint

Avoid:

- cheap mockup collage
- gloomy dark theme
- overused crosses
- clipart
- putting the logo everywhere
- crowded product grids
- random fonts
- bright yellow everywhere

## Homepage Sections

1. Header
2. Hero
3. Brand story
4. Launch capsule grid
5. Investor/vision section
6. Footer

## Hero Direction

The hero should make Radiant 34 immediately feel like a real brand. The visual should feel like light breaking through darkness, not a church event flyer.

Core hero line:

Faith-made apparel with light in the fabric.

## Product Direction

Product cards should feel like early capsule placeholders until real photography and mockups are ready. Do not use fake low-quality product images. Use premium abstract product cards first, then replace with real mockups later.

## Typography Direction

- Large compressed-feeling display type through tight letter spacing
- Clean sans body copy
- Uppercase micro labels
- No decorative religious fonts

## Next Upgrade

Replace abstract cards with generated or photographed product mockups:

- T-shirt
- Hoodie
- Tank top
- Bracelet
- Key chain
- Water bottle
- Backpack

Mockups must match the radiant black/cream/gold system.
