# Radiant 34 Product Roadmap

## Launch Capsule

The first launch should be small, clear, and premium. Do not launch with too many random designs.

### Product 1: Signature T-shirt

Purpose: Main entry product.

Design direction:

- Cream or black heavyweight tee
- Small Radiant 34 chest mark
- Larger back artwork inspired by Psalm 34:5
- Use sun rays, light, and clean typography

### Product 2: Psalm 34:5 Hoodie

Purpose: Higher-value hero product.

Design direction:

- Heavyweight hoodie
- Minimal front
- Strong back design
- Scripture phrase used carefully, not overcrowded

### Product 3: Radiant Tank Top

Purpose: Everyday summer/gym product.

Design direction:

- Clean front mark
- Optional small Psalm 34:5 reference
- Light, radiant feel

### Product 4: Bracelet

Purpose: Low-cost faith accessory.

Design direction:

- Debossed or engraved Radiant 34
- Optional phrase: Never Covered With Shame
- Black, gold, cream, or natural tones

### Product 5: Key Chain

Purpose: Simple add-on product.

Design direction:

- Radiant 34 mark
- Psalm 34:5 on reverse side
- Metal, rubber, or acrylic options

### Product 6: Water Bottle

Purpose: Functional lifestyle product.

Design direction:

- Clean vertical logo or 34 mark
- Avoid large overdesigned artwork
- Premium black, cream, or brushed steel

### Product 7: Backpack

Purpose: Higher-ticket lifestyle product.

Design direction:

- Minimal external branding
- Small patch or woven label
- Inner scripture detail as hidden message

## Design System Rules

1. Do not put the main logo on every product.
2. Use scripture and symbols with restraint.
3. Keep the brand premium and readable.
4. Use no crown of thorns in the main logo system.
5. Prioritise mockups that look investable.
6. Product photos should feel bright, warm, and editorial.

## Product Page Structure

Each product should have:

- Product name
- Short spiritual meaning
- Material/fit information
- Product story
- Size guide where needed
- Product images
- Lifestyle image
- Detail image
- Care instructions

## Future Expansion

After launch:

- Kidswear
- Church group orders
- Mission fundraiser products
- Artist collaborations
- Limited scripture capsules
- Premium cut-and-sew pieces
- Devotional card packs
- Gift bundles
